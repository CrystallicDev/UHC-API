## Senarios

Here is a list of existing scenarios. When they are platform-specific, it will be mentionned. 

### 1.7 Enchants
> Enchanting is reverted to how it works in MC 1.7, where it takes all the levels it costs, you cannot preview what you are getting and no lapis is required.

### Random Crafts
> Every crafts yields a random item. A craft will always yield the same item.

### Random Loot
> Every looted item (block breaking, mobs, chests, etc) will be randomized. Loot will always loot the same items.

### Random Mobs
> Every mob type is randomised. (ex: Skeletons become Slimes in the overworld)

### Loot Crates
> Every X minutes, a configurable amount of lootcrate will fall on random spot of the world.

### Revive
> 3 small structures appears in random places of the world. Coordinates are broadcasted at the start of the game. Players can revive their dead team mates in exchange of some valuable items.

### Monsters Inc
> Going through a door will teleport players to another door in the world randomly

### Bookception
> Killing a player will drop a randomly Enchanted Book

### Super Heroes
> Every member of the team will receive a random super power in : <br>
> **Strength I** <br>
> **Speed I** <br>
> **Resistance II** <br>
> **Jump Boost IV + Speed I + Fire Resistance + Saturation + NoFall** <br>
> **Health Boost V** <br>

### Kings
> At the begining of the game, all players will be able to vote for a King in their own team. The king will obtain the following : <br>
> **Speed I** <br>
> **Strength I** <br>
> **Resistance I** <br>
> **Health Boost II** <br>
> **Haste II** <br>
> If the King dies, all the team members suffer from Poison II effects for 90 seconds.

### Wheel of Fortune
> Every X minutes, a random player will be selected to receive a random bonus or malus. The bonus / malus will be broadcasted in the chat.
